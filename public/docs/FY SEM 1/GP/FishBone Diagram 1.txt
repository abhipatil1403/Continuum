Problem: Weak Communication Skills


Lack of Confidence

Fear of making mistakes while speaking
Low self-esteem leading to hesitation
Overthinking how others will perceive your speech

Limited Practice

Not engaging in regular conversations with diverse people
Not seeking feedback or opportunities for improvement
Not setting aside time for communication exercises

Unclear Expression

Struggling to organize thoughts before speaking
Using filler words (e.g., “um,” “like”) too often
Speaking too fast or too slow, causing confusion

Listening Skills

Difficulty focusing while others are speaking
Interrupting others before they finish their thoughts
Not practicing active listening or summarizing points

Language Barriers

Limited vocabulary or lack of fluency in a language
Inability to express thoughts clearly in non-native language
Overthinking grammar and structure during conversations

Non-Verbal Communication

Not using hand gestures or facial expressions effectively
Difficulty maintaining an engaging tone of voice
Lack of awareness of personal space during conversations
