Problem: Anger Issues


Emotional Triggers

Difficulty controlling reactions to criticism
Frustration from unmet expectations
Sensitivity to personal or professional setbacks
Overreacting to minor inconveniences

Stress Management

High levels of stress from work or studies
Inability to relax during tense situations
Overcommitting to tasks, leading to burnout
Lack of healthy outlets for stress relief (e.g., exercise, hobbies)

Communication Barriers

Struggling to express feelings constructively
Misunderstandings leading to heated arguments
Avoiding open conversations, resulting in bottled-up emotions
Difficulty empathizing with others during conflicts

Lack of Self-Control

Acting impulsively without thinking of consequences
Difficulty pausing to reflect before reacting
Struggling to manage emotions during heated moments
Overwhelming sense of frustration leading to outbursts

External Factors

Stressful environments (e.g., noisy, chaotic settings)
Negative influence from peers or situations
Frequent exposure to conflict or hostility
Lack of a supportive system to talk through feelings

Mental and Physical Health

Fatigue or lack of sleep affecting patience levels
Underlying anxiety or mood-related issues
Unhealthy lifestyle choices contributing to irritability
Lack of techniques like mindfulness or meditation
