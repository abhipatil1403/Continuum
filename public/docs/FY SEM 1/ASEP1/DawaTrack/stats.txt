1. Total Medicines Taken (Daily, Weekly, Monthly)
Chart Type: Line Chart or Bar Chart
Line Chart → Best for showing trends over time.
Bar Chart → Useful if comparing different periods (e.g., Jan, Feb, Mar).

2. Most Frequently Taken Medicine
Chart Type: Pie Chart or Horizontal Bar Chart
Pie Chart → Shows the proportion of each medicine taken.
Horizontal Bar Chart → Easier to read if there are many medicines.

3. Upcoming Expiry Alerts
Chart Type: Timeline Chart or Bar Chart
Timeline Chart → Shows medicine names on a timeline with their expiry dates.
Bar Chart → Display medicines along the x-axis and days left before expiry on the y-axis.

4. Stock Usage Trends (How fast a medicine is used)
Chart Type: Line Chart or Area Chart
Line Chart → Displays how stock decreases over time.
Area Chart → Gives a more visual impact by shading the area under the line.

5. Most Prescribed Medicines
Chart Type: Bar Chart or Pie Chart
Bar Chart → Better for comparing different medicines.
Pie Chart → Good for showing distribution.

6. Doctor Visit Frequency
Chart Type: Column Bar Chart or Line Chart
Column Bar Chart → Easy to see how often a user visits a doctor.
Line Chart → Shows the frequency trend over time.
