DawaTrack Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how DawaTrack ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our medicine reminder application.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, age, gender, and contact information provided during account creation and profile setup.
  • Medical Information: Medicine names, prescription details, dosage information, and medical appointment schedules.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the application, including reminder settings and preferences.
  • Calendar Data: With your permission, we access your Google Calendar to create medicine and appointment reminders.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our medicine reminder service
  • To send reminder notifications about medicines and appointments
  • To create and manage calendar events for your reminders
  • To improve our application and user experience
  • To communicate with you about your account and updates

Data Storage and Security

We use Supabase for secure data storage and management. All data is encrypted and stored securely. We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access or disclosure of your information.

Google Calendar Integration

When you use our Google Calendar integration:

  • We only request access to create and manage events related to your medicine reminders
  • We don't access or read your existing calendar events
  • You can revoke our calendar access at any time through your Google Account settings

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties except as necessary to provide our services (such as Google Calendar integration) or as required by law.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us at dawatrack.official@gmail.com

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
