Frequently Asked Questions

You can register for sports events through our Events page. Each event listing has a registration button that will guide you through the process. Alternatively, you can contact our Events team at events@khelconnect.gov.in.

The Sports Department offers various development programs for athletes at different levels. Please contact our Athlete Support team with details about your sport, achievements, and requirements. They will guide you through the available programs and eligibility criteria.

Media organizations can apply for press credentials by contacting our Media & Press department at least 14 days before the event. Please include your organization details, the event you wish to cover, and the names of team members requiring access.
