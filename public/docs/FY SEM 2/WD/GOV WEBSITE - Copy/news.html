India to Host 2026 Commonwealth Games

In a major boost to India's sporting aspirations, the Commonwealth Games Federation (CGF) has officially announced India as the host nation for the 2026 Commonwealth Games.

This marks the second time India will host the prestigious multi-sport event, following the successful Delhi Games in 2010. The event is expected to be held across multiple cities including New Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru, showcasing India's world-class sporting infrastructure.

May 15, 2025 | Sports Department
