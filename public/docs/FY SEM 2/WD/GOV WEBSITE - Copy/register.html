By registering for this event, you confirm that all information provided is accurate and complete. Participants must meet all eligibility requirements specified for the event.

2. Cancellation Policy

Cancellations made more than 14 days before the event are eligible for a full refund. Cancellations made between 7-14 days before the event are eligible for a 50% refund. No refunds will be issued for cancellations made less than 7 days before the event.
