Virat Kohli
Sport: Cricket
Role: Batsman
Born: November 5, 1988
State: Delhi
Biography

Virat Kohli is an Indian international cricketer and former captain of the Indian national team. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest batsmen of all time in the history of cricket. Between 2013 and 2022, he captained the India cricket team in more than 200 matches across all three formats.

Career Highlights
  • 71 International centuries
  • ICC Cricketer of the Year multiple times
  • Fastest to reach 10,000 ODI runs
  • Led India to historic Test series win in Australia
Recent Performances

Virat Kohli continues to be a key player for the Indian cricket team across all formats. His consistency and ability to perform under pressure make him one of India's most valuable players.
