About DSC VIT

Developer Student Clubs (DSC) is a Google Developers program for university students to learn mobile and web development skills, design thinking, and leadership skills.

Technical Skills

Learn the latest technologies like Android, Cloud, Machine Learning, and more through hands-on workshops and projects.

Community

Connect with like-minded students, build your network, and collaborate on projects that solve local problems.

Innovation

Develop solutions for local businesses and communities while applying design thinking and problem-solving skills.
