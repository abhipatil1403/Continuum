Frequently Asked Questions

How can I join GDSC VIT Pune?

You can join GDSC VIT Pune by attending our events and workshops. Follow our social media channels for updates on recruitment drives.

Do I need prior experience to attend workshops?

No, our workshops are designed for all skill levels. We have beginner-friendly sessions as well as advanced topics.

Are GDSC events free to attend?

Yes, all GDSC events are free for VIT Pune students. Some events might require prior registration.

How can I collaborate with GDSC?

We welcome collaborations with other student organizations and industry partners. Please reach out to us through the contact form.
